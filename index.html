The range

Doors and windows — built to spec

Aluminium Doors

Precision-engineered entrance doors, single or double, framed for a considered, symmetrical façade.

Sliding Doors

Smooth-running sliding doors on 18mm nylon rollers — the everyday workhorse for opening a home outward.

Barn Doors

Sliding barn-style doors on an exposed track, for a bold, industrial-inspired opening.

Shower Doors

SmartGlass-endorsed safety glass, self-closing with a magnetic anti-drip seal for a watertight fit.

Top Hung

Hinged at the top and swinging outward, for ventilation without sacrificing weatherproofing.

Side Hung / Casement

Hinged along the vertical edge for full-opening ventilation and an unobstructed view.

Sliding

Low-maintenance and space-efficient — the standard choice for offices and everyday living areas.

Butt Jointed

Bolt-jointed with no visible corner frames — a clean, continuous line of glass ideal for staircases.

Arched

Custom-bent frames for curved openings, manufactured to the architect's exact radius.

Tilt & Turn

European-designed with a dual opening mechanism — tilt for ventilation, turn for full access.
